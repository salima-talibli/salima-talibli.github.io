Scoring Framework

Green-space need — 25%
Distance from existing parks.

Urban heat — 20%
Local Landsat land-surface temperature within a 90 m context.

Parking lot size — 20%
Larger surface-parking sites receive greater redevelopment potential.

Built-up intensity — 15%
Building-footprint coverage within a 150 m local context.

Transit accessibility — 10%
Distance to the nearest HVV public-transport stop.

Land-value feasibility — 10%
Normalized Hamburg Bodenrichtwert used as a relative feasibility proxy.
